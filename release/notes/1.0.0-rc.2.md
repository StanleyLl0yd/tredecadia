# Tredecadia 1.0.0-rc.2

This second v1 release candidate makes one intentional compatibility-critical correction before stable `v1.0.0`: the seven regular weekday positions now have internationally neutral canonical identities and names.

Canonical weekday identity is:

`W1 Mene · W2 Noko · W3 Kese · W4 Zoyo · W5 Sote · W6 Yemo · W7 Toze`

The weekday names use a two-syllable `CVCV` system with the weekday-only `E/O` vowel space. Machine IDs `W1..W7`, order, canonical ASCII names, and ordered two-syllable identities are frozen for RC2 review.

The calendar registry schema advances from version `1` to version `2` because `regularGrid.weekdays` changes from seven English strings to seven structured canonical weekday records.

Everything else in the frozen calendar identity is retained from RC1: the 13×28 grid, month identities and abbreviations, `EQ`/`ED`, Tredecadia Era and year zero, epoch, leap rule, civil conversion, canonical date grammar, month pronunciation policy, and reviewed month-localization maps.

The published `v1.0.0-rc.1` release remains immutable and reproducible at its original tag, commit, archive, and checksum. RC2 is a new prerelease rather than a rewrite of RC1.

The existing reviewed `ru-Cyrl`, `ja-Kana`, and `ko-Hang` localization profiles continue to cover month aliases only. RC2 does not invent unreviewed localized weekday aliases.

After publication, RC2 enters a new minimum one-full-day observation period before stable `v1.0.0` may be considered.
