# Tredecadia 1.1.0

Tredecadia 1.1.0 is the stable compatible v1.1 localization release, promoted from the published `v1.1.0-rc.1` after the required observation interval completed with zero recorded compatibility-critical findings and zero open RC feedback reports.

The canonical v1 calendar identity is unchanged from stable `v1.0.0`: 13 × 28 regular structure, Tredecadia Era and epoch, `EQ`/`ED` semantics, Gregorian conversion and leap rules, date grammar, all 13 canonical month names and abbreviations, and all seven canonical weekday IDs/names (`W1 Mene` through `W7 Toze`).

The following six independently reviewed month-display localization profiles are promoted to **stable** maturity:

- `ka-Geor` — Georgian Mkhedruli
- `hy-Armn` — Eastern Armenian
- `ar-Arab` — fully vocalized Arabic
- `hi-Deva` — Hindi-oriented Devanagari
- `bn-Beng` — Bengali
- `fa-Arab` — Iranian Persian

The existing stable Russian (`ru-Cyrl`), Japanese (`ja-Kana`), and Korean (`ko-Hang`) profiles remain unchanged. All six newly stable profiles preserve their exact reviewed RC mappings; no native-speaker usability study is claimed. There are no localized weekday aliases in this release.

Only public version/status metadata and the accepted profiles' maturity statuses change in the normative registries. The published `v1.0.0` and `v1.1.0-rc.1` tags and release artifacts remain immutable. The release archive and `SHA256SUMS` are produced deterministically and verified by the guarded GitHub Actions publishing workflow.
